# customer-shopping-behaviour-analysis-2 📊

### Turning Retail Data into Actionable Business Insights

## 📌 Project Overview

**Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis** is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding retail purchasing patterns, customer segments, product performance, discount usage, and market opportunities.

The project uses SQL to analyze customer shopping data and Power BI to transform analytical findings into interactive dashboards. The results are presented through a business-focused report and a stakeholder-ready presentation.

The objective is to identify meaningful patterns in customer purchasing behaviour, understand what contributes to recorded purchase value, and translate data-driven insights into practical business recommendations.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze overall purchase performance using key metrics.
* Identify the products and categories contributing the most to recorded purchase value.
* Understand purchasing patterns across customer segments.
* Compare purchasing behaviour between subscribers and non-subscribers.
* Evaluate discount and promotional usage patterns.
* Identify locations with potential opportunities for increasing purchase value.
* Explore customer loyalty, purchasing frequency, seasonality, and operational preferences.
* Develop actionable recommendations supported by analytical evidence.

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains **3,900 customer purchase records and 18 columns** covering several aspects of retail shopping behaviour.

The major data categories include:

* **Customer Information:** Customer ID, age, gender, and subscription status.
* **Product Information:** Purchased item, category, size, and colour.
* **Purchase Behaviour:** Purchase amount, previous purchases, and purchase frequency.
* **Location & Season:** Customer location and season.
* **Promotions:** Discount application and promotional code usage.
* **Operations:** Shipping type and payment method.
* **Customer Feedback:** Review ratings.

The dataset supports analysis of purchasing patterns, customer segmentation, product performance, and differences in recorded purchase value.

**Data note:** The original spreadsheet is raw and may contain inconsistencies. The numerical findings reported in the completed analysis and results presentation should be treated as the project's analytical outputs. The raw spreadsheet should not be considered a separately validated source of results.

## 💡 Selected Business Insights

The completed analysis identifies several findings that can help guide further business investigation:

* **Recorded purchase value:** The dataset represents approximately **$233,081** in total purchase value.
* **Average purchase amount:** The average purchase amount per record is approximately **$59.76**.
* **Category contribution:** Clothing and Accessories account for approximately **76.5% of recorded purchase value**.
* **Customer segments:** Differences in total purchase value should be interpreted alongside the number of records and average purchase amounts.
* **Promotional activity:** The observed average purchase amount differs between discount and non-discount records. This comparison alone does not establish whether discounts increase or decrease incremental sales.

These findings describe the available data. They do not independently establish profitability, causation, or the commercial impact of a proposed strategy.

## 📈 Business Recommendations

Based on the analytical findings, the project identifies opportunities for further investigation:

1. **Category Cross-Selling:** Explore complementary product recommendations and bundles within categories that contribute a large share of recorded purchase value.

2. **Localized Basket-Building:** Investigate targeted product bundles and relevant offers in locations where customer volume and average purchase amount indicate potential opportunities.

3. **Customer-Segment Recommendations:** Test relevant product recommendations across customer segments and compare the results using measurable performance indicators.

4. **Promotion Evaluation:** Use controlled experiments to evaluate promotional effectiveness before expanding discount campaigns.

5. **Performance Monitoring:** Track purchase value, average purchase amount, customer volume, and category contribution through Power BI dashboards.

These recommendations should be evaluated through controlled pilots before being adopted at scale.
